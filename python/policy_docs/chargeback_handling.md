# Chargeback Handling Policy

## 1. Chargeback Categories

### 1.1 Fraud-Related Chargebacks
- **Unauthorized transaction**: cardholder did not authorize the transaction.
- **Counterfeit card**: transaction made with a cloned or counterfeit card.
- **Card-not-present fraud**: online or phone transaction made without cardholder's knowledge.

### 1.2 Non-Fraud Chargebacks
- **Goods not received**: customer did not receive the purchased item.
- **Defective merchandise**: product significantly different from description.
- **Duplicate charge**: customer charged more than once for the same transaction.

## 2. Timeframes

### 2.1 Cardholder Filing
- Cardholder must dispute within 120 days of the transaction date.
- For recurring charges, within 120 days of the expected delivery date.

### 2.2 Investigation Timeline
- Initial review: within 2 business days of receiving the dispute.
- Provisional credit: issued within 10 business days if investigation is ongoing.
- Final resolution: within 45 calendar days (90 days for international transactions).

### 2.3 Merchant Response
- Merchant has 30 days to respond with compelling evidence.
- If no response, chargeback is automatically finalized in the cardholder's favor.

## 3. Evidence Requirements

### 3.1 For Fraud Chargebacks
- Transaction authorization records (AVS, CVV verification).
- IP address and device fingerprint (for online transactions).
- Delivery confirmation with signature.
- Communication logs with the cardholder.

### 3.2 For Non-Fraud Chargebacks
- Proof of delivery (tracking number, delivery confirmation).
- Product description and photographs.
- Terms of service and return policy acknowledged by customer.
- Refund or replacement records.

## 4. Internal Processing

1. Log the chargeback in the dispute management system.
2. Assign to the appropriate team (fraud vs. non-fraud).
3. Gather evidence within 5 business days.
4. Prepare the representment package.
5. Submit response to the card network.
6. Track outcome and update records.

## 5. Metrics and Monitoring

- Monitor chargeback ratio: must stay below 1% of total transactions.
- Track win rate for representments.
- Identify merchants with high chargeback rates for review.
- Report trends monthly to risk management.
