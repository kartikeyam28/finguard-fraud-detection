# Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Policy

## 1. KYC Tiers

### Tier 1: Simplified Due Diligence (SDD)
**Applies to**: Low-risk customers with accounts under $5,000 balance.
**Requirements**:
- Full name and date of birth.
- Government-issued ID number.
- Residential address.
- Verification: automated database check.

### Tier 2: Standard Due Diligence (CDD)
**Applies to**: All standard account holders.
**Requirements**:
- All Tier 1 requirements, plus:
- Source of funds declaration.
- Employment or business information.
- Expected account activity profile.
- Verification: document-based (ID copy, proof of address less than 3 months old).

### Tier 3: Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
**Applies to**: High-risk customers (see triggers below).
**Requirements**:
- All Tier 2 requirements, plus:
- Detailed source of wealth documentation.
- Purpose and nature of business relationship.
- Senior management approval for onboarding.
- Ongoing monitoring with quarterly reviews.
- Verification: independent source verification, site visits where applicable.

## 2. EDD Triggers

Apply Enhanced Due Diligence when any of the following conditions exist:
- Customer or beneficial owner is a Politically Exposed Person (PEP).
- Customer is from a high-risk jurisdiction (refer to FATF high-risk and grey list).
- Complex or unusual ownership structures.
- Cash-intensive business (restaurants, retail, ATM operators).
- Customer's transaction patterns deviate significantly from their profile.
- Fraud score consistently above 0.6 on multiple transactions.
- Customer has been previously associated with suspicious activity.

## 3. Ongoing Monitoring

### 3.1 Transaction Monitoring
- All customers: automated transaction monitoring per the Transaction Monitoring Rules.
- Tier 2: monthly review of transaction patterns against expected activity.
- Tier 3: weekly review, dedicated analyst assignment.

### 3.2 Periodic Reviews
- Tier 1: every 5 years.
- Tier 2: every 3 years.
- Tier 3: every 12 months.

### 3.3 Trigger-Based Reviews
Conduct an immediate review when:
- A SAR is filed involving the customer.
- Customer's fraud score exceeds 0.8 on any transaction.
- Significant change in transaction volume or pattern.
- Adverse media or law enforcement inquiry.

## 4. Record Keeping

- All CDD documentation must be retained for 5 years after the end of the business relationship.
- Electronic copies are acceptable provided they meet regulatory standards for authenticity.
- Access to CDD records must be restricted to authorized compliance personnel.
